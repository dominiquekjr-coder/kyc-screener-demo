Run a name screen against open sources

Enter the legal name of the prospective or existing client. The system runs two structured open-source queries against Google — one for adverse news, one for political exposure and associations — and returns a numbered, classified summary of the first twenty results from each.

Use the legal name as it appears on government-issued identification. Diacritics and aliases may be added in a follow-up review.
01

Adverse news query

Targets fraud, laundering, sanctions, corruption, criminal proceedings and related coverage across mainstream and specialist publications.

02

PEP & associations query

Targets political exposure, state ownership, ministerial appointments, and corporate or family affiliations relevant to enhanced due diligence.

03

Classification & reporting

Every result is numbered, classified into one of five compliance categories, and aggregated into an overall risk verdict for the subject.

For demonstration use. Open-source screening is a starting point, not a substitute for sanctions list checking, ID verification, or analyst review. Outputs require human adjudication before any decision is taken.
